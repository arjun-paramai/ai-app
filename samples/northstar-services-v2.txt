MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT - VERSION 2

1. Parties and term
This agreement is between Northstar Analytics LLC (Provider) and Harbor Retail Inc. (Customer). The effective date is January 1, 2026. The initial term ends December 31, 2026. It renews for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written non-renewal notice at least 90 days before the current term ends.

2. Services and payment
Provider will deliver monthly sales analytics reports and maintain the reporting portal. Customer will pay $6,500 per month. Invoices are due within 15 days of receipt. Customer must provide access to source data within five business days of a reasonable request.

3. Confidentiality
Each party will protect the other party's confidential information with reasonable care and use it only to perform this agreement. These obligations survive termination for five years. Information already public without breach is excluded.

4. Termination
Either party may terminate for a material breach that remains uncured 15 days after written notice. Customer may terminate for convenience with 90 days' written notice and must pay an early termination fee equal to three months of service fees.

5. Liability
Provider's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid during the preceding three months. Customer's payment obligations are not subject to a liability cap. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages. The cap does not apply to fraud.

6. Data and deletion
Customer owns its source data. Provider may process it solely to provide the services. Provider must delete Customer data within 60 days after termination and confirm deletion in writing. Provider must notify Customer of a confirmed data breach within 96 hours of discovery.

7. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the laws of Illinois. The parties will first attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiation.
